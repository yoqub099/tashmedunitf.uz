# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone — regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Standards

**Examples of positive behavior:**
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting differing viewpoints
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the project

**Examples of unacceptable behavior:**
- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language
- Personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported to the project tech lead at **conduct@tashmedunitf.uz**.
All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
